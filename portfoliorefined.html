02 / ABOUT ME

A little About me.

Hello, I’m Yashvi.

A UI/UX designer in the making, currently at MIT Institute of Design, Pune originally from Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

My interest in design didn't start with a course, it started with a habit: I've always noticed why things feel awkward before I can explain what's wrong with them. A confusing form, a button in the wrong place, a flow that makes you think twice I clock it instantly, and then I can't stop thinking about how I'd fix it.

That instinct is what pulled me toward UX. Design, for me, isn't decoration it's the difference between someone using a product and someone fighting it. Outside of design, I'm the person who wants to know why people behave the way they do which is probably why half my projects start with "why do people actually do this?" instead of "what should we build?" That, and a slightly competitive obsession with board game mechanics.
