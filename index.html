02 / ABOUT ME

A little About me.

Hello, I’m Yashvi.

A UI/UX Designer in the making, currently in my third year at MIT University, Pune originally from the vibrant city of Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

My journey into design started long before I knew what UX even meant. Growing up with a love for digital art, I was always drawn to how visuals could evoke emotion and tell stories. That curiosity naturally led me to UI/UX where creativity meets logic and every decision has a purpose behind it.

I care deeply about building experiences that feel intuitive and human. For me, good design isn’t just about how something looks it’s about how it works and how it makes people feel.
