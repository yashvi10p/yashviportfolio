02 / ABOUT ME

A little About me.

Hello, I’m Yashvi.

I’m a final-year UI/UX design student at MIT Institute of Design, Pune, driven by a deep fascination with human psychology and how we interact with technology.

I’m the kind of designer who listens first. I want to understand what quietly frustrates someone, how the smallest micro interactions shape decisions, and where an interface starts getting in the user's way. For me, design is about taking complex, messy problems, finding creative solutions, and putting them in front of real people to hear what they actually think.

When I’m away from Figma, I’m usually experimenting in 3D spaces, picking up new tools, sketching out rough ideas, or getting lost in music.

What kind of problems do I like solving?

tap to flip

I enjoy problems where the solution isn’t obvious at first. Especially when there’s a gap between what users want to do and what a system expects them to do.

How do I approach a new design problem?

tap to flip

I try not to jump straight into designing. I first understand the problem, the people involved, and the context around it. I research, ask questions, look for patterns, and then define what actually needs to be solved. Only after that do I start exploring solutions, testing them, and iterating.
